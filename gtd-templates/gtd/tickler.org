#+TITLE: Tickler — future-scheduled
#+STARTUP: overview

Items to surface on a specific future date. Use =SCHEDULED:= or
=DEADLINE:= timestamps. They appear in the daily agenda on/after
the scheduled date.

Examples:
- "Renew passport — review options" SCHEDULED in 6 months
- "Check on the kitchen reno quote" SCHEDULED next week
- Recurring reminders: =SCHEDULED: <2026-01-01 +1y>=
