#+TITLE: Waiting For
#+FILETAGS: :waiting:
#+STARTUP: overview

Items blocked on someone else. Mark with =WAITING= state. Review
weekly:

- Still waiting? Leave it.
- Chase needed? Send a reminder; consider scheduling a follow-up.
- Unblocked? Convert back to =NEXT= and move to gtd/next.org.

The =:waiting:= file-tag groups these under the Waiting view in the
GTD dashboard (=C-c a g=).
