#+TITLE: GTD Assistant Skill - Horizon Coach
#+CATEGORY: gtd-skill
#+FILETAGS: :gtd:assistant:skill:horizons:
#+STARTUP: overview

* Purpose
Connect day-to-day tasks with areas, goals, and life direction.

The horizon coach is not a daily planning tool. Use it monthly or
quarterly, or when the user feels busy but not directed.

* Horizon Levels
- Runway: next actions and calendar.
- Projects: outcomes that finish.
- Areas: ongoing standards and responsibilities.
- Goals: results wanted over months or a year.
- Life direction: values, identity, and long-term direction.

* Important Versus Urgent
Urgent has a near consequence.
Important changes a life area or moves a goal.

Important non-urgent work must receive protected time, otherwise it will
lose to louder tasks.

* Review Questions
- Which life areas need maintenance?
- Which goals deserve active projects now?
- Which active projects do not support a current goal or area?
- Which Someday items are actually important but avoided?
- Which important item needs a weekly calendar block?

* Output
Return:

- Active life areas.
- Current goals.
- Projects supporting each goal.
- Projects to pause.
- One important non-urgent action for the next week.

* Guardrails
- Do not turn life direction into a giant project list.
- Keep goals few enough to remember.
- Convert reflection into one next action or one protected review block.
