#+TITLE: GTD Assistant Skill - Initial Setup Coach
#+CATEGORY: gtd-skill
#+FILETAGS: :gtd:assistant:skill:setup:
#+STARTUP: overview

* Purpose
Build, or rebuild, the system from zero. Once. Get every open loop out of
the head and into trusted buckets so the daily and weekly views can stay
small. This is the capture-and-clarify pass that the weekly review assumes
has already happened.

* When To Use
- The first time GTD is set up.
- After a long gap, when the lists no longer reflect reality.
- When the weekly review keeps surfacing "this is outdated" and trust is gone.

Run this rarely (a few times a year at most). Day-to-day drift is the
weekly review's job, not this.

* Process
1. Capture (mind sweep): empty the head into inbox.org. Do not organize yet.
2. Clarify: process inbox top to bottom, one item at a time.
3. Organize: file each clarified item into its bucket.
4. Reflect: set the review rhythm so the system stays trusted.

Capture fully before clarifying. Mixing the two stalls both.

* Mind Sweep Triggers
Walk these out loud and write everything that pulls attention into
inbox.org. Raw phrasing is fine; do not decide anything yet.

Professional / projects:
- started but unfinished, promised but not delivered, waiting on others,
  decisions owed, things to read, write, learn, or fix.

Personal / life:
- health, fitness, sleep, food; finances, taxes, bills, subscriptions;
- home, repairs, cleaning, things to buy, replace, or throw away;
- family, friends, calls owed, gifts, events; admin, documents, renewals;
- car, travel, errands; hobbies, creative work, ideas parked too long.

Open mental loops:
- anything you keep re-remembering; anything that creates vague guilt;
- every "I should...", "I need to...", "what about..." — capture the phrase.

* Clarify Rules
For each captured item, decide in order:

1. Actionable? If no, then trash, reference, or someday/maybe.
2. One action or multi-step? More than one action means it is a project.
3. Under two minutes? Do it now, do not file it.
4. Yours to do? If not, then waiting.org with who and when.
5. Date-bound? A hard date or time goes to calendar.org. Otherwise next.org.
6. A project goes to projects.org with an outcome line and a concrete NEXT.

Write the next physical, visible action, not the outcome. "Call X about Y",
not "sort out Y".

* Project Rules
Every project needs a one-line outcome (what "done" looks like) and at
least one NEXT action that is concrete and doable now. A project without a
NEXT is stuck before it starts.

* Output
Return:
- A clean inbox (nothing left unclarified).
- Count of projects created, each with its NEXT.
- Items moved to waiting, someday, reference, or calendar.
- 1 to 3 projects to make active first; the rest can wait their turn.
- Anything that is really an Area or a Horizon, flagged for later rather
  than forced into projects.

* Guardrails
- Capture without judging or solving. Speed first, decisions later.
- Do not turn the mind sweep into doing the tasks.
- It is fine, and normal, for most captured items to become someday or trash.
- Do not define life goals here. Clear the runway first; horizons later.
- Stop when the head feels empty, not when the list looks impressive.
